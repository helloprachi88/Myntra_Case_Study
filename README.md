# Myntra Pricing Strategy Case Study

## Project Overview

This project is a Business Analysis case study focused on understanding why products on Myntra are often priced higher than competing e-commerce platforms and how pricing strategy impacts customer behavior and business performance.

The objective of this project was to analyze pricing differences, study customer purchasing behavior, identify root causes behind premium pricing, and recommend solutions to improve competitiveness while maintaining brand positioning.

---

## Business Problem

Customers frequently compare prices across multiple e-commerce platforms before making purchase decisions.

A common customer concern is that similar products on Myntra are often priced higher compared to other marketplaces.

This creates several business challenges:

* Customers abandon carts after comparing prices
* Customers switch to competitor platforms
* Conversion rates decline
* Price-sensitive customers lose trust in pricing fairness
* Customer retention becomes difficult

The business objective is to understand the reason behind higher pricing and improve competitiveness without affecting brand value.

---

## Project Objective

The main objective of this project was to:

* Study Myntra’s pricing strategy
* Compare product pricing with competitors
* Analyze customer behavior and survey insights
* Identify factors contributing to premium pricing
* Measure impact on important business KPIs
* Recommend solutions to improve customer retention and competitiveness

---

## Business Analysis Approach

The project was completed using the following structured approach.

### Step 1 — Problem Identification

Identified customer concerns related to higher product pricing compared to competitor platforms.

### Step 2 — Competitor Benchmarking

Compared product pricing across major e-commerce platforms: Myntra, Amazon, Flipkart

### Step 3 — Customer Survey Analysis

Studied customer purchase priorities using a survey of 500 online shoppers.

Important factors analyzed:

* Price sensitivity
* Discount preference
* Brand trust
* Delivery expectations
* Return convenience

### Step 4 — KPI Analysis

Evaluated impact on important business metrics.

Key KPIs analyzed:

* Conversion Rate
* Cart Abandonment Rate
* Repeat Purchase Rate
* Customer Retention Rate
* Revenue Growth

### Step 5 — Root Cause Analysis

Identified major factors responsible for higher pricing.

Key causes:

* Premium brand positioning
* Higher operational cost
* Reverse logistics cost
* Marketplace pricing strategy
* Marketing and customer support overhead

### Step 6 — Solution Recommendation

Recommended strategic solutions to improve price competitiveness.

---

## Key Findings

Important observations identified:

* Products were priced 25% to 50% higher than competing platforms
* 72% customers consider price as the most important buying factor
* Higher pricing directly increases cart abandonment behavior
* Premium pricing negatively impacts conversion and retention

---

## Recommended Solutions

Proposed business solutions:

* Dynamic Pricing Engine
* Price Match Guarantee
* Better Pricing Transparency
* Personalized Discounts for repeat customers
* Operational Cost Optimization

---
